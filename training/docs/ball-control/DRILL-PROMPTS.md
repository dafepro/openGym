# QuickTrack drill prompts and separate captions

Each block below is ready to append to the unchanged master prompt in
[VIDEO-PRODUCTION.md](VIDEO-PRODUCTION.md#pasteable-master-prompt). Choose the front
or oblique45 camera recipe in that master. Attach the same approved references.
Every motion time below is measured from clip start, with no cuts. These are
original demonstration cadences, not exercise prescriptions. All clips remain
planned. A prompt's detail is not proof that its generated motion is correct.

Right and left always mean the athlete's anatomical sides. Front-facing video
shows the athlete's right on image-left. Forward means away from the athlete and
backward means toward the athlete, regardless of camera angle. Do not mirror.
A ball-width is a relative visual guide, not a claim about mat dimensions. Reduce
travel to fit the actual reference mat while keeping each contact readable.

Caption copy is plain text for an accessible player/transcript or later editorial
track. It is deliberately outside generation prompts. It is not a currently
implemented subtitle import format. Do not ask the generator to draw it. Manual
count definitions below are an optional consistent convention for the human;
manual entries remain self-reported, and camera arrivals never inherit those units.

## `bc-side-roll` — Side-to-side sole rolls

Shots: `bc-side-roll-front-v1`, `bc-side-roll-oblique45-v1`.

```text
DRILL: bc-side-roll. Demonstrate alternating side-to-side SOLE rolls, not passes.
Start 0–2 s: adult faces forward; ball rests on the floor just ahead and right
of the midpoint between the shoes. Feet on floor, shoulder-width comfortable
stance, soft knees. Both side endpoints lie just in front of the shoes and stay
within easy reach. Keep ball path left/right, not forward/back.
2–3.3 s: weight on left floor foot. Put right sole lightly onto ball top and roll
the ball across the front of the body toward athlete-left. Right shoe travels
with the rolling ball; no sideways kick. Release sole pressure at left endpoint.
3.3–3.5 s: weight shifts to right foot on floor; left sole collects the ball.
3.5–4.8 s: left sole rolls it back along the same lane toward athlete-right.
4.8–5 s: release; settle into the original ball endpoint and balanced stance.
Repeat that exact right-sole-left, left-sole-right cycle at 5–8, 8–11, 11–14 s.
Small support steps are allowed; do not cross feet, stand on the ball or jump.
14–16 s: ball settled at original athlete-right endpoint, both shoes on floor,
matching start pose. No additional touches. Show natural light ball pressure.
```

Separate caption copy:

- 0–2: “Side-to-side sole rolls. Start slowly with soft knees.”
- 2–8: “Right sole rolls left. Left sole rolls right. Shift weight to the floor foot.”
- 8–14: “Keep the ball close and on the floor. Use light sole pressure.”
- 14–16: “Camera feedback counts target arrivals; it does not verify technique.”

Review focus: ball rolls under a moving sole rather than sliding under a hovering
shoe; each return uses the other foot. For real camera practice, place target A/B
at the two observed ball endpoints in the live preview; use no invented mat grid.

## `bc-push-pull` — Forward/back sole rolls

Shots: `bc-push-pull-front-v1`, `bc-push-pull-oblique45-v1`.

```text
DRILL: bc-push-pull. Demonstrate forward/back SOLE rolls using the right foot.
This is NOT the laces-forward pull-push variant. Do not add a kick or change feet.
Start 0–2 s: face forward, ball stationary just in front of right shoe, left foot
on floor a comfortable distance beside the ball lane. Both feet initially down.
The forward endpoint is reachable without leaning; backward endpoint is in front
of the right heel, never underneath the left support foot.
2–3.3 s: transfer weight to left foot. Right sole touches ball top and draws the
ball backward toward the body along a straight short lane. Ball rotates on floor.
3.3–3.5 s: lightly settle at backward endpoint without loading body onto ball.
3.5–4.8 s: the same right sole rolls ball forward to its original endpoint; extend
the ankle/leg only within comfortable reach. Do not strike with toe or laces.
4.8–5 s: lift right sole off; right shoe returns to floor beside original stance.
Repeat the exact back-then-forward cycle at 5–8, 8–11, 11–14 s. Only right foot
contacts ball throughout this take. Left support knee remains soft and stable.
14–16 s: both feet on floor and ball settled at original forward endpoint.
```

Separate caption copy:

- 0–2: “Forward/back sole rolls. Ball just ahead of one foot.”
- 2–8: “Use the same sole to roll toward you, then away. Keep the range short.”
- 8–14: “Stay on the floor foot. Pause and reset before practicing the other foot.”
- 14–16: “Camera feedback counts target arrivals, not touches or complete repetitions.”

Review focus: the near/far difference must be visible in front view and confirmed
in oblique view. Calibrate A/B at actual image endpoints, which must be separated
enough for valid targets. Do not promise a particular physical distance or camera
accuracy. The clip's all-right-foot choice is deliberate; a left-foot derivative
would require a new reviewed asset rather than automatic mirroring.

## `bc-toe-taps` — Alternating toe taps

Shots: `bc-toe-taps-front-v1`, `bc-toe-taps-oblique45-v1`.

```text
DRILL: bc-toe-taps. Light alternating forefoot-SOLE touches on top of a nearly
stationary ball. Walking rhythm, not a fast airborne hopping sequence.
Start 0–2 s: face forward, ball centered just ahead of shoes, both feet on floor.
2–2.75 s: weight on left foot. Lift right forefoot, touch ball top lightly with
sole beneath the toes, release, return right shoe fully to the floor.
2.75–3.5 s: weight on right foot. Left forefoot sole touches ball top, releases,
then left shoe returns to floor.
3.5–4.25 s: repeat right touch-and-return.
4.25–5 s: repeat left touch-and-return. Ball stays at the same floor location;
it may rock slightly from a real light contact but cannot drift or bounce away.
Repeat this four-touch cycle at 5–8, 8–11, 11–14 s: right, left, right, left.
One floor foot supports the body at all times. Never stand with body weight on
ball, stamp it, strike its front with toe tip, or jump with both feet airborne.
14–16 s: both shoes on floor behind ball, exactly as at start; ball still.
```

Separate caption copy:

- 0–2: “Alternating toe taps. Keep the ball almost still.”
- 2–8: “Touch the top with the sole under your toes, then put that foot down.”
- 8–14: “Alternate right and left. Touch lightly; do not stand on the ball.”
- 14–16: “Optional manual count: one light top touch is one repetition.”

Review focus: soles contact ball top; toe tips do not drive it forward. Both feet
must remain distinct during every change of support. This is timed/manual mode;
ball movement is not used to infer tap repetitions.

## `bc-inside-touches` — Inside-to-inside touches

Shots: `bc-inside-touches-front-v1`, `bc-inside-touches-oblique45-v1`.

```text
DRILL: bc-inside-touches. Small alternating inside-foot nudges, no sole contact.
Start 0–2 s: ball on floor between feet, slightly nearer the right foot, forward
of the heel line; knees soft, enough space for the ball to travel between shoes.
2–2.75 s: right toes turn slightly outward; broad inside of right shoe nudges ball
left across the short lane. Left shoe receives with its inside, softly limiting
travel. Right shoe returns to support without crossing over ball.
2.75–3.5 s: inside of left shoe nudges ball right; right inside receives softly.
3.5–4.25 s: right inside nudges left again.
4.25–5 s: left inside nudges right; settle near original right-side position.
Repeat the four alternating nudges at 5–8, 8–11, 11–14 s. Use small natural floor
steps and weight shifts; keep ball rolling on floor between feet, no toe pokes,
sole traps, scoops, kicks, airborne phases or spreading the feet into a split.
14–16 s: softly settle the last ball arrival using right inside surface, then
place both shoes on floor around the ball, matching starting arrangement.
```

Separate caption copy:

- 0–2: “Inside-to-inside touches. Ball between your feet.”
- 2–8: “Right inside sends left. Left inside sends right. Keep touches small.”
- 8–14: “Use short balance steps and the broad inside of each shoe.”
- 14–16: “Optional manual count: each deliberate inside nudge is one repetition.”

Review focus: distinguish propulsive nudges from soft receiving contacts; do not
make a rendered contact count an app metric. Ball must not pass through the toes
when the receiving shoe rotates.

## `bc-v-pull` — V pulls

Shots: `bc-v-pull-front-v1`, `bc-v-pull-oblique45-v1`.

```text
DRILL: bc-v-pull. Alternating sole pull, then same-foot INSIDE push on a new
forward diagonal. Ball draws two legs of a small V, not a straight back-and-forth.
Start 0–2 s: face forward; ball ahead-right, both feet down and balanced behind it.
2–3.2 s: weight on left floor foot. Right sole draws ball diagonally backward
from ahead-right toward the central space just ahead of the body. Keep ball clear
of the support shoe. This is the rear vertex of the V.
3.2–3.5 s: lift right sole free, turn right toes outward and bring the INSIDE of
right shoe to the ball's athlete-right side. No ball movement without contact.
3.5–4.7 s: right INSIDE nudges the ball diagonally forward-left to an easily
reachable endpoint; this push uses the same foot as the preceding sole pull.
4.7–5 s: right shoe returns to floor; left foot prepares to collect, no hidden reset.
5–6.2 s: left sole draws from ahead-left diagonally back to central vertex.
6.2–6.5 s: release left sole; turn foot and present left INSIDE.
6.5–7.7 s: left inside nudges forward-right. 7.7–8 s: settle into starting stance.
Repeat the right-foot 2–5 s cycle at 8–11 s and left-foot 5–8 s cycle at 11–14 s.
Use four complete pull/push units total: right, left, right, left. Keep floor
contact, small range and distinct sole release before every inside push.
14–16 s: ball stationary ahead-right with both feet down, matching start pose.
```

Separate caption copy:

- 0–2: “V pulls. Start with the ball diagonally ahead.”
- 2–8: “Pull back with your sole. Lift, turn, then push forward with the same foot's inside.”
- 8–14: “Change the forward angle. Mirror with the other foot.”
- 14–16: “Optional manual count: one pull plus its inside push is one repetition.”

Review focus: two surfaces, same foot per pull/push; the forward leg must visibly
change angle. The V vertex stays in front of the athlete, not under a planted foot.

## `bc-l-pull` — L pulls

Shots: `bc-l-pull-front-v1`, `bc-l-pull-oblique45-v1`.

```text
DRILL: bc-l-pull. Small sole draw-back followed by same-foot INSIDE push behind
the support heel. Show every recovery roll; no teleport or cut. Do not do a V.
Start 0–2 s: face forward; ball ahead-right. Left support shoe is beside and left
of ball's backward lane; its heel line leaves a clear ball-width passage behind.
Both feet on floor, enough clear space behind heels and beside both shoes.
2–3 s: left foot supports, knee soft. Right sole pulls ball straight backward in
the lane to athlete-right of the left shoe, until ball is fully BEHIND left heel.
3–3.6 s: right sole releases; turn right toes outward. INSIDE of right shoe nudges
ball sideways to athlete-left BEHIND the left support heel. The ball path makes
an L: back first, then across. Ball cannot intersect heel or ankle.
3.6–4 s: right shoe returns to floor slightly behind/right for support; small
repositioning steps are visible and do not step into the ball. Left sole collects
the ball at athlete-left, behind the previous heel line.
4–4.7 s: left sole rolls ball FORWARD along athlete-left side, clear of both shoes,
to a reachable ahead-left start position. This visible roll is recovery, not the L.
4.7–5 s: release left sole; both shoes reset facing original forward direction.
5–6 s: MIRROR: right foot supports beside lane; left sole pulls straight back on
athlete-left until ball fully clears behind right support heel.
6–6.6 s: left sole releases; left INSIDE pushes ball right behind right heel.
6.6–7 s: left foot settles for support; right sole collects ball at athlete-right.
7–7.7 s: right sole rolls ball forward along athlete-right into original start.
7.7–8 s: release and reset both shoes, no cut or unexplained displacement.
Repeat the right-foot 2–5 s sequence at 8–11 s, then left-foot 5–8 s at 11–14 s.
14–16 s: both feet down; ball ahead-right at original position, fully stationary.
Never twist on a locked support knee, hop over the ball, clip the support ankle,
or let the front-view overlap conceal an impossible path. Oblique view must show
the behind-heel path and sole-to-inside release without camera movement.
```

Separate caption copy:

- 0–2: “L pulls. Start slowly with space behind your heels.”
- 2–8: “Sole pulls back past the support heel. Same-foot inside sends the ball across behind it.”
- 8–14: “Collect and roll forward to reset. Then mirror. Back first, then across.”
- 14–16: “Optional manual count: one back-and-across L is one repetition; recovery is not counted.”

Review focus: this is the hardest contact/occlusion shot. Reject a plausible-looking
front clip if frame review cannot establish a physically clear behind-heel path.
If necessary, rerecord a real competent adult rather than accepting generated
footwork with uncertain contact. Approval is never inferred from the prompt.

## `bc-inside-outside` — Inside/outside touches

Shots: `bc-inside-outside-front-v1`, `bc-inside-outside-oblique45-v1`.

```text
DRILL: bc-inside-outside. Use only the RIGHT foot's inside and outside in this
take. Left foot supports with small floor steps. No alternating-foot return.
Start 0–2 s: ball stationary just ahead-right, right shoe near its right side;
left support foot slightly behind-left, soft knees, both feet initially down.
2–3.1 s: turn right toes outward and nudge ball left a short distance with broad
INSIDE of right shoe. Ball stays in front of athlete, clear of the support foot.
3.1–3.6 s: lift and reposition right shoe to the ball's left side, turning toes
slightly inward to present OUTSIDE edge. Show the actual foot arc above floor;
ball continues naturally then slows through friction, with no invisible touch.
3.6–4.7 s: OUTSIDE of the same right shoe nudges ball right toward start endpoint.
4.7–5 s: right shoe resets near ball's right side; small left support step allowed.
Repeat the right-inside-left / right-outside-right cycle at 5–8, 8–11, 11–14 s.
Keep ball lane short and slightly ahead of both shoes. Do not flick it airborne,
strike with toe tip, rotate a planted knee, or let left foot return the ball.
14–16 s: softly stop at original ahead-right endpoint with right inside if
needed, then both shoes on floor in matching start pose. Do not add another cycle.
```

Separate caption copy:

- 0–2: “Inside/outside touches. Start with one foot.”
- 2–8: “Inside nudges across. Reposition, then outside of the same foot returns it.”
- 8–14: “Take small balance steps. Stop and reset before changing feet.”
- 14–16: “Optional manual count: one inside-and-outside pair is one repetition.”

Review focus: the outside contact is the outer shoe edge, not an impossible inside
surface on the wrong side of the ball. This all-right-foot clip must not be
mirrored automatically to imply a separately approved left-foot demonstration.

## `bc-stepover` — Inside step-overs

Shots: `bc-stepover-front-v1`, `bc-stepover-oblique45-v1`.

```text
DRILL: bc-stepover. INSIDE step-over preparation around a STATIONARY ball.
Outside-to-inside foot arc; no outside-to-outside scissors, ball touch or exit kick.
Start 0–2 s: face forward; ball centered a short distance in front of the shoes,
both feet on floor just behind ball, balanced with soft knees.
2–3.2 s: weight on left floor foot. Lift right foot and swing it from OUTSIDE-RIGHT
around the FRONT (far side) of ball toward athlete-left, clearing the ball without
touching it. Low controlled arc, no leap; ball remains completely stationary.
3.2–3.7 s: place right foot on floor to ball's left; legs briefly crossed, knees
soft and body balanced. Small natural body/shoulder shift toward athlete-left.
3.7–5 s: uncross with a small visible right-foot step backward around the ball,
returning to original behind-ball stance. Left foot may make a small balance step;
no fixed-knee twist, ball touch, jump, turn, or hidden reset.
5–6.2 s: mirror with left foot moving from OUTSIDE-LEFT around FRONT of ball to
athlete-right, while right floor foot supports.
6.2–6.7 s: place left foot on floor to ball's right, briefly crossed and balanced.
6.7–8 s: visibly step back to uncross and regain starting stance.
Repeat the right 2–5 s unit at 8–11 s and left 5–8 s unit at 11–14 s.
The ball never moves. All foot placements and clearance must remain visible.
14–16 s: both feet down behind still ball, exactly matching original balanced pose.
```

Separate caption copy:

- 0–2: “Inside step-overs. Ball stays still.”
- 2–8: “Circle from outside to inside around the front. Clear the ball without touching it.”
- 8–14: “Step back to uncross and reset. Then mirror with the other foot.”
- 14–16: “Optional manual count: one completed foot arc and reset is one repetition.”

Review focus: the inward arc must be distinct from an outward scissors action.
No soccer acceleration is demonstrated in this confined-space preparation. This
is timed/manual mode because a stationary ball cannot reveal a correct stepover.
